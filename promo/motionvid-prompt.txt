Cut to dark navy background (#0a0f1e). A warm light beam shines down, and the MizanNet app icon (attached) animates in with a bouncy scale, glowing soft blue. Below it, bold white text: "MizanNet" (with "Net" in orange #FF8A3D) and tagline: "SAHANIN KONTROLÜ TEK EKRANDA". Uplifting chord hit.

SCENE 3 (8-13s) — DASHBOARD:
Slide in the real dashboard screenshot (attached: dashboard.png) as a floating window with soft shadow, gentle Ken Burns zoom. Caption at bottom: "Nakit akışı, kâr, depo — hepsi anlık 📊". Highlight the revenue chart with an animated orange line trace effect.

SCENE 4 (13-17s) — CARİ HESAPLAR:
Transition to the current accounts screenshot (cari.png). An animated orange counter badge pops in top-right counting up from 0 to "₺12.500.000" labeled "TOPLAM BAKİYE". Caption: "Cari hesaplar tek listede, excel derdi bitti". Satisfying coin/count sound.

SCENE 5 (17-21s) — FLEET & WORKERS:
Quick snap-cut: vehicles screenshot (araclar.png) slides from right, then workers screenshot (isciler.png) slides up over it slightly smaller, creating a layered stack. Caption: "Filo bakımı + maaş bordrosu — tek dokunuş 🚛👷". Snappy whoosh sounds on each snap.

SCENE 6 (21-25s) — AI ASSISTANT:
Show the AI assistant screenshot (asistan.png). Animated chat bubbles appear over the empty chat area: first a blue user bubble "Faturayı yükle 📄", then a dark AI reply bubble "Toplam: ₺48.750 · KDV dahil ✓ Kategoriye işlendi" typed out letter by letter. Caption: "Yapay zeka faturayı tanır, raporu yazar 🤖". Soft keyboard typing + success ding.

SCENE 7 (25-30s) — CLOSING CTA:
Dark navy background returns. The app icon scales in with glow, "MizanNet" wordmark below, orange tagline: "SAHANIN DİJİTAL BEYNİ". Subtitle: "Verileriniz yerelde · Çevrimdışı çalışır · Tamamen Türkçe". Then an orange pill button pops in: "⬇ HEMEN İNDİR". Confident, warm outro music fade.

STYLE: Modern SaaS motion graphics, dark navy theme (#0a0f1e) with orange (#FF8A3D) and blue (#2196F3) accents, rounded corners, soft glows, smooth cubic-bezier easing, Ken Burns zooms on screenshots. Narration in Turkish (male, confident, warm). Energetic corporate background music.
